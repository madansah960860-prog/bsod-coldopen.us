# Image credits

Every image referenced by this site is stored in this repository. Nothing is
hot-linked from a third-party server at runtime.

All photographs below were released under **CC0 1.0 Universal** or the
**Public Domain Mark**, which permit commercial use without attribution. They
are credited anyway, because crediting photographers is the right thing to do.
They were located through [Openverse](https://openverse.org), the Creative
Commons search index, and downloaded from the original host.

Each photo was centre-cropped, resized to 400 / 800 / 1600 px wide, re-encoded
as WebP, and stripped of EXIF metadata. No file exceeds 150 KB.

No screenshot, logo, character, box art or other trademarked material belonging
to any game publisher appears anywhere in this repository.

## Original artwork — not sourced from stock

These were drawn for this project and are original work. No third-party rights apply.

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `assets/img/og/og-*.png` | Twelve Open Graph cards. Generated composition: a seeded frame-time graph, the COLD OPEN mark, and the house palette. One per page. |
| `assets/img/avatars/*.png` | Abstract portrait marks for bylines. These are geometric compositions, **not photographs**, and are deliberately not pictures of real people — the staff described on the About page are fictional, and using a stranger's photograph to represent them would be misleading. |
